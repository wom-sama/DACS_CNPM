## Blocking findings

The verifier could not resolve 20 mandatory source-derived registry families
without additional frozen model/tensor/formula schemas. It therefore could not
complete either public API. Behind that hard stop, review also found latent
false-PASS paths: self-declared repository receipts; opaque claim, quota,
handoff, transition and operational-root records; S2B reads before a verified
immutable S2A transition; incomplete nested-array/CIDT/scientific validation;
and ONNX/TensorRT parsing without complete ORT/TRT output-parity and deployment
decisions.

The launcher could be invoked directly in child mode with a caller-created
receipt, did not pin the pytest dependency surface, and could neither admit the
verifier's lazy TensorRT imports nor prove that no external dependency loaded a
GPU package before its post-run check. Its intended test source did not yet
exist. Consequently, neither exact-eight collection nor a green result could
have carried authority.

The combined pushed schema plus rejected verifier/launcher activation surface
reached 3,086 nonblank lines: schema `1,225`, rejected verifier `1,261` and
rejected launcher `600`, against the prospective near-3,200-line ceiling, while
no S1/S2 producer existed. Under this branch's frozen budget and one-attempt
stop rule, fixing the findings would require new control schemas, resolvers and
runtime files rather than a bounded correction. Further v2 activation work is
therefore classified as protocol drift for this branch, not as new science.
