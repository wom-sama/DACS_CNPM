## Purpose and bounded claim

This protocol asks one narrow question before any trainer integration:

> Does a scratch-only, validity-aware Pair-Surface Decoupled Filter (PSDF)
> sidecar add stable class-1-versus-0/2/4 surface information beyond the frozen
> keeper margin and matched learned controls, while protecting class-1 true
> positives?

PSDF is the presentation name of the already specified composition: two
sample-conditioned, spatial/channel-factorized DDF blocks, four pair-specific
evidence/attention heads and a keeper-margin calibrator. DDF, dynamic filtering,
pairwise learning and attention are prior operators. The possible contribution
is therefore a scratch-only, validity-aware composition and its causal evidence
for mango maturity, not a claim that the DDF operator itself is new.

Passing this gate would establish train-only conditional mechanism evidence.
It would not establish a new production model, validation superiority, test
generalization or a paper-confirmatory result. Those require a separately
locked integration experiment and a newly sealed source/time/site cohort.

## Cohort and access boundary

The exact train-only cohort remains 763 rows drawn from targets 0, 1, 2 and 4,
with five held folds of 152-153 rows. It is grouped into 158 transitive
source-family/numeric-neighborhood components. Per-fold targets remain 31-32
class 0, 108-109 class 1, 10-11 class 2, zero class 3 and exactly two class 4.
Class-4 results are mandatory safety vetoes, not positive generalization
claims. The folds do not prove fruit/session independence because no trusted
fruit/session identity exists.

The fold assignment and its aggregate target counts are already frozen,
label-aware design metadata. Here `target-blind` means that no per-row target
array, target slice or target-derived statistic is an input to donor, offset,
bootstrap, initialization, order or raw-output construction. The parent exposes
only a typed sample-to-component/fold projection to those functions.

The future authorization must bind exact bytes plus member identities for the
retained 763-row cache, its masks/boxes/keeper probabilities, the clean 9,215-row
CIDT train table, V2R2 folds and every source used by the runner. Only those
train-only inputs may be opened. Raw images/labels, validation, project test,
manual-review labels, external predictions/features and keeper checkpoint
forward passes are forbidden.

Whole-container hashing is provenance only; it does not authorize every member.
The authorization must list each deserialized member, dtype, shape, semantic
role and SHA-256. Paths and members not listed are denied. The historical
project test remains retrospective and cannot be made confirmatory by a new
ledger.

The reviewed parent, all ten fold workers and the scientific finalizer are

## Target-blind release order

One external parent consumes the combined authorization and follows this
irreversible order:

1. Verify the reviewed authorization, pushed source commit, exact registry,
   environment, resources, absent create-once output/claim and input identities.
2. Spawn the primary fold-0 child without targets. It loads only structural/
   non-target members, constructs validity, component, donor, offset, bootstrap,
   initialization and epoch-order states, returns their target-blind root over
   the parent channel and waits.
3. The parent writes that immutable target-blind root. Only then does the trusted
   parent decode the bound 763-row target member and materialize exact
   create-once fit/calibration/held shards. This is trusted-parent dataflow and
   worker isolation, not a claim that the parent is physically ignorant of
   target bytes. The parent cannot import model/scientific code at this stage.
4. Release only fold 0's typed fit-target shard to the waiting child. It trains
   all five roles, freezes weights and fit-only masked-BN, produces calibration,
   held, ablation and causal raw outputs without calibration or held targets,
   then exits.
5. Repeat step 4 in fold order 1-4. Before receiving its fit shard, every fresh
   child independently recomputes and matches the immutable target-blind root;
   every role starts from its locked seed in a new model/optimizer instance. A
   later fold cannot access or rewrite an earlier target buffer, target-blind
   root or fitted state.
6. Finalize the five-fold primary raw root, then launch five fresh replay fold
   children. Every replay child independently recomputes the target-blind root
   before receiving its fit shard; all five must equal the primary root. They
   then reproduce the fit/raw-output roots. Workers never open calibration,
   held or CIDT targets.
7. Only after both raw roots are immutable, spawn one scientific-finalizer
   child. It opens the five calibration-role target shards for primary and
   replay. Across five outer folds their union contains all 763 target bytes;
   this is explicitly trusted-finalizer visibility, not physical target
   secrecy. Leakage control is instead source-enforced role indexing: for each
   outer fold, `_fit_all_calibrators` may index only that fold's declared
   calibration rows, never its held rows. The finalizer separately calls the
   pinned `_fit_all_calibrators` and `_apply_derived_actions` for primary and
   replay, then writes two create-once pre-held bundles. Each bundle contains
   the complete direct outputs of both calls: `union_probabilities`,
   `pair_probabilities`, identities for 250 named calibrators, ten fold-level
   action selections, `action_probabilities`, and two label-blind
   `AppliedAction` objects whose four arrays each are committed. Together the
   bundles commit 500 calibrators, 20 selections, six probability arrays, four
   applied-action objects and all 16 arrays contained by those objects before
   any held-role attribution or CIDT release.
8. The finalizer then verifies the held-role shard identities and their
   per-fold provenance. This transition does not reveal new A0 target bytes,
   because the calibration union already covered all rows; it authorizes only
   held-role attribution. Only afterward does it open CIDT target/baseline
   arrays once. It builds the complete typed primary and replay runs, derives
   held/CIDT evidence and requires every refitted direct output for each run --
   union, pair and action probabilities; calibrators; selections; and both
   applied actions with all contained arrays -- to equal its own persisted
   pre-held bundle bit-for-bit. CIDT cannot feed back into fit, calibration,
   threshold or action selection.
9. The finalizer derives all gates and raw-replay comparisons in one atomic
   result. The parent verifies its manifest/root without importing scientific
   code. No fold worker or caller can write the decision root.

The runner must never accept a caller-provided PASS, metric, count, digest root,
fold mapping, donor mapping, threshold or transition decision. It recomputes
them from the bound bytes. Targets may supervise their declared fit/calibration
operations but cannot enter donor construction, head selection, held action
selection or any preceding target-blind artifact. Calibration, held and CIDT
targets are attribution inputs owned by the staged finalizer, never raw evidence
emitted by a producer. The strong claim is that fold workers cannot decode
non-fit targets and selection functions receive only stage-typed views; it is
not cryptographic isolation from the reviewed parent that owns data release.

## Raw evidence and scientific decision

The pinned v2 public `evaluate_exact_a0_conjunctive_gates` is deliberately
unavailable and its `LineageLock` requires the superseded S1/S2 roots. IG1 must
not call that hard-stopped API, call the development-only synthetic evaluator,
or fabricate v2 stage identities.

A new compact `pair_surface_psdf_ig1_scientific.py` defines an honest
`IG1LineageLock`, a strict raw parser and the sole exact IG1 entry point. It may
reuse only these six hash-pinned numerical internals from the retained
scientific module: `_fit_all_calibrators`, `_apply_derived_actions`,
`_validate_run`, `_derive_run`, `_gates_1_to_13` and
`_build_comparison_records`. Their exact source SHA freezes behavior despite
their private names. Before held-role attribution and CIDT release, the first
two functions operate separately on typed primary and replay calibration-role
views. Each view necessarily contains all 763 target bytes because the five
calibration folds partition the table; the adapter proves that each preimage
can index only its declared calibration positions. The only permitted public
objects from that module are frozen schema data types required by those
functions:
`FrozenArrayIdentity`, `FrozenTargetSlice`, `TargetShardLock`,
`NamedArtifactIdentity`, `SidecarFoldPreimage`, `TrainingTraceEvidence`,
`RawScientificRun`, `CalibratorState`, `NamedCalibratorState`, `ActionPolicy`,
`ActionSelection`, `AppliedAction`, `ActionMetrics`, `BootstrapResult`,
`MapStatistics`, `GateResult`, `RepeatActionComparisonV2`,
`CIDTDiagnosticResult`, `DerivedRunEvidence`, `ScientificComparisonRecord` and
`ConjunctiveGateEvaluationV2`. A frozen dataclass is not treated as deep
immutability: before any pinned call or persistence, the adapter makes every
contained NumPy array contiguous, sets `writeable=False`, and commits dtype,
shape, bytes and SHA-256. The two pre-held bundles are create-once persisted
bytes, not merely frozen Python objects. Their methods may be used; no
additional scientific callable is allowed.

The exact staged call sequence is performed independently for `run_name` in
`("primary", "replay")` before held-role attribution or CIDT release:

1. `(union[run_name], pair[run_name], calibrators[run_name],
   selections[run_name]) = _fit_all_calibrators(calibration_role_run[run_name])`;
2. `(action_probabilities, primary_action, repeat_action) =
   _apply_derived_actions(calibration_role_run[run_name], union[run_name],
   selections[run_name])`, then persist that run's complete create-once bundle;
3. after both bundles exist, verify held-role provenance, then release CIDT and call
   `_validate_run(primary, ig1_lock, name="primary", exact_a0=True)` and the
   same for replay;
4. call `_derive_run(run, ig1_lock)` separately for primary/replay, then
   `_gates_1_to_13(run, derived)` for each;
5. call `_build_comparison_records(ig1_lock, primary, replay,
   primary_derived, replay_derived, primary_gates, replay_gates)` once.

Only `_validate_run` receives `exact_a0=True`. The adapter first validates IG1
authorization, target-blind, primary, replay, target-shard, CIDT and artifact
roots. It requires each run's final union/pair/action probability arrays,
calibrators, selections and both applied actions (including all eight contained
arrays) to equal that run's create-once pre-held bundle bit-for-bit and retains
the same 69-record gate-14 comparison semantics. Editing the v2 scientific
source, using its v2 lineage validator or duplicating its metric equations is
forbidden.

Workers serialize only raw arrays/states/identities. After both raw roots close,
the fresh finalizer child imports the adapter and recomputes all calibrators,
metrics, actions and gates as the independent consumer. The adapter rejects
missing, extra, caller-derived or non-finite fields. Its output cannot
retroactively change a worker artifact.

All 14 retained clean gates remain conjunctive. In compact form they require:

1. `KUP-K` pooled AUROC at least `0.015`, bootstrap lower 95% bound above zero;
2. `KUP-K` pooled AUPRC at least `0.005`, bootstrap lower bound nonnegative;
3. DDF KUP AUROC at least `0.015` above static and `0.010` above each trained
   single-factor control;
4. DDF beats keeper and static in at least four of five folds with positive
   median deltas;
5. class 1-vs-0/2 pair safety/superiority and class 1-vs-4 keeper-minus-0.005
   safety exactly as frozen;
6. numerical pair attribution `KUP-KUH` at least `0.005`, nonnegative in four
   folds and positive in median;
7. support-matched self stability, capacity-balanced cross-fold substitution
   degradation, non-wrap displacement degradation and exact invalid-fill
   invariance;
8. clean raw union beats both same-weight neutral-factor ablations by `0.010`;
9. at least 97% of 528 keeper class-1 TPs retained, at least 45/222 restricted
   FPs rejected and corrections at least twice harms plus neutral changes;
10. the CIDT train-table diagnostic improves class-1 precision by `0.030`,
    class-1 F1 by `0.015` and macro F1 by `0.003`, with recall loss at most
    `0.030`;
11. repeat AUROC within `0.010`, repeat passes the action gate, eligible-row
    action agreement at least `0.97` and suppressed-set Jaccard at least `0.80`;
12. the exact bbox/attention/evidence localization and dispersion thresholds;
13. per-block spatial and channel sample dispersion at least `1e-4`;
14. every state finite and primary/replay raw and derived evidence exact.

Outcomes are exhaustive:

- `information_pass`: all gates pass in primary and exact replay;
- `scientific_fail`: complete valid evidence exists but one or more gates fail;
- `harness_fail_before_observation`: failure occurs before any optimizer step or
  candidate score; retain a tombstone and require a new reviewed source commit
  and authorization for a bounded correction;
- `invalid_run_after_observation`: any harness/identity/access failure after an
  optimizer step or score; retain and close IG1 without retry.

No qualitative score or reviewer vote can override a failed gate.

## Deployment gates frozen before A0

These gates are frozen now but remain unauthorized until `information_pass`.
They must all pass before integration or promotion, and A0 accuracy cannot
relax them.

The isolated export wrapper is exactly the engine `forward()` contract, not a
future trace wrapper. Inputs are `images` FP32 `[B,3,64,64]`, `valid64` bool
`[B,1,64,64]`, `valid32` bool `[B,1,32,32]` and `valid16` bool
`[B,1,16,16]`; the only output is `scores` FP32 `[B,4]`. Use `ddf_full` and
`static_matched` primary fold-0 final states. The workload is a 32-row NumPy
PCG64 sequence: create `Generator(PCG64(2026072901))`, call
`standard_normal((32,3,64,64))` once in FP64, then cast C-contiguously to
`dtype('<f4')`. For row index `i`, `valid64` is true only on
